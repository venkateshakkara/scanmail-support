ScanMail Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

ScanMail does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

On-device processing

All document scanning, text recognition, and summarization happen entirely on your device. Your documents and images are never uploaded to any server.

Data retention

ScanMail does not save your documents. Scanned pages, recognized text, and summaries are kept only in memory while you view them, and are discarded when you start a new scan or close the app.

AI summaries

Summaries are generated automatically on your device and may contain mistakes. Always check important details, such as amounts and dates, against the original document.

Sharing

Documents are only sent when you choose to email or share them, using your own Mail account or the sharing apps you select.

Camera and Photos

ScanMail uses the camera only to scan documents you choose to capture, and accesses only the photos you select.

No tracking

ScanMail contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and does not require an account.

Children's privacy

ScanMail is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if new features are added in a future version, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
