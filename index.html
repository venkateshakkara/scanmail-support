ScanMail scans receipts and documents, reads the text, summarizes every detail, and emails it with a PDF of the pages.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I see a "basic summary"?
AI summaries use Apple Intelligence. On devices where it's off or unsupported, ScanMail creates a basic summary of dates, totals, phone numbers, and addresses instead.

The Email button says Mail isn't available.
Set up an account in the Mail app, or use Share Summary to send it another way.

Text wasn't read correctly.
Scan in good lighting with the whole page in view, and keep the camera steady.
